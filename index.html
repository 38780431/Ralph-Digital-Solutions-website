QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know
before we begin.

A few helpful details about requesting support, project scope and the examples shown on this site.

How do I request a service?

Choose the closest service in the enquiry form and describe what you need. Your email app will open with a message for you to review and send.

Do you work with individuals and organisations?

Yes. The services are intended for individuals, startups and growing organisations. We can discuss the requirements and agree on a suitable scope.

Can I combine services in one project?

Yes. For example, a project may combine a website, business email and staff guidance. Tell us your goals and we can discuss the pieces together.

Are the work examples completed client projects?

No. The visuals in the homepage slideshow and work section are illustrative capability concepts, not representations of completed client projects.

Can you guarantee data recovery or search rankings?

No. Data recovery depends on the condition of the device and data, and search visibility depends on many factors outside our control. We can explain the scope and limitations before work begins.

Are hosting, domains and paid software included?

No. Third-party subscriptions, domains, hosting and paid software are billed separately by their providers. We can discuss the required setup before you commit.

Does the chat assistant use AI or save my messages?

No. It uses built-in guided answers in your browser. It does not send or store your chat messages.
